Every text has a unique fingerprint.
Your text is converted into a SHA-512 hash, then mapped to digits 0-9. Each digit becomes a colored dot on a 32×32 grid. Adjacent dots with the same digit connect automatically. No two inputs will ever produce the same image — with more possible patterns than atoms in the observable universe, your Memorabilia is truly one of a kind.
